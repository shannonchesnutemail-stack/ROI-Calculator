Disclaimer
There is no guarantee you will hit the exact lead/revenue numbers shown. Even with a verified 3% landing page conversion benchmark and real Acculynx funnel rates, expect a margin of swing of roughly ±20% due to market-to-market variance and inconsistent closing ratios (especially in newer markets like Houston and New Orleans). Use this calculator as a planning indicator, not a guarantee. KPIs (CPL, conversion rate, funnel rates) are refreshed every 30 days as real campaign data comes in.
