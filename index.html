Why CPL Matters
Cost Per Lead (CPL) is what you pay to generate one lead. It sets how many leads a given budget can buy (Leads = Ad Spend ÷ CPL) and drives every result below it — revenue, profit, and ROI. A CPL above your Max Affordable CPL means this scenario loses money at your current margin and close rate.
What CTR Affects
Click-Through Rate (CTR) does not lower your cost per lead directly — CPC already captures that. CTR is Google's Quality Score / impression-share signal: a weak CTR raises CPC over time and shrinks how many impressions your budget reaches. Treat it as an ad-relevance health check, not a revenue lever.

Disclaimer
There is no guarantee you will hit the exact lead/revenue numbers shown. Even with a verified 3% landing page conversion benchmark and real Acculynx funnel rates, expect a margin of swing of roughly ±20% due to market-to-market variance and inconsistent closing ratios (especially in newer markets like Houston and New Orleans). Use this calculator as a planning indicator, not a guarantee. KPIs (CPL, conversion rate, funnel rates) are refreshed every 30 days as real campaign data comes in.
