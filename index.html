Methodology

Adjusted CPL = Your CPL × (Channel Benchmark CTR ÷ Your CTR)

First-Time Leads/Month = Monthly Ad Spend ÷ Adjusted CPL (new roof projects only)
Closed Jobs = Leads × Lead Approval Rate × Approval Close Rate
Break-even CPL = Ticket × Margin × Overall Close Rate
Headroom = Break-even CPL − Adjusted CPL
